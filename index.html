Profile

I am a Ph.D. candidate researcher specializing in reliable AI, open-world computer vision, and explanation reliability, with a focus on models that know when to predict, defer, or reject under domain shift. During my doctoral studies, I developed a three-paper reliability program spanning open-world SAR ATR, tri-source reliability fusion, and calibrated explanation gating. I am most interested in building AI systems that are honest about what they do not know — from radar target recognition to astronomical transient triage.
